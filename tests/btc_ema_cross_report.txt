METHODOLOGY
================================================================================
  WIN     : TP hit before SL — no time limit
  LOSS    : SL hit before TP — no time limit
  ONGOING : Trade still open at report generation time
  UNKNOWN : Data fetch error

  EMA computed with standard multiplier k = 2/(period+1)
  Seeded with SMA of first `period` closes
  Crossover: strict change in relative position of EMA9 vs EMA26
  Entry at candle CLOSE (not open of next candle)
  SL/TP checked from the candle AFTER entry close forward
